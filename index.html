1. Midnight Racing & Circuit Derbies

Illegal motor derbies are a lucrative avenue for converting dirty cash into cold hard bankrolls. Races are decided by two key components: Driver Attributes and Vehicle Tuning.

  • Driver Formula: Driving attribute accounts for 55% of cornering capability, while Aim/Muscle acts as aggressive defensive blocking against competitors attempting to run your car off the embankment.
  • High-Bore V8 Modification: Adds a flat +35 Speed bonus, vastly improving straightaway lead potential on the Lake Shore Drag Strip.
  • Derby Risks: Intense dirt tracks (such as the Cook County Mud Derby) carry a 12% engine blowout hazard. If your car is damaged, the driver risks hospitalization unless equipped with heavy armor plating.
