1. The Dual Currency Economy: Dirty Cash vs. Clean Bank Vault

In 1926 Chicago, money is split into two distinct tiers: Dirty Street Cash and Clean Bank Balance.

  • Dirty Street Cash: Yielded directly from underground rackets (Speakeasies, Gambling Dens, Brothels, Protection Shakedowns). Used for street soldiers' weekly upkeep, black-market Tommy Guns, alleyway mob surgeries, and constructing raw illicit operations.
  • Clean Bank Balance: Deposited in legitimate civic banking institutions. Required for municipal bribes, retaining Cook County Judges, buying the Police Commissioner, retaining appellate defense lawyers, and purchasing legitimate commercial property fronts.
  • The IRS Audit Hazard: If your Dirty Cash reserves skyrocket while your laundering network remains weak, the Federal Internal Revenue Bureau will flag your outfit, resulting in immediate confiscation warrants and coordinated padlocking of premises.

4. Fortifications, Bouncers & Armed Guards

Unfortified rackets are sitting ducks for rival raids and rogue police precinct shakedowns. Upgrading a plot unlocks defensive tiers:

  • Level 1 (Standard): Unreinforced doors. Standard vulnerability to raids and vandalism.
  • Level 2 (Reinforced Steel Doors & Bouncers): Costs $2,500. Increases income/capacity by 40% and adds +35% defense against rival incursions.
  • Level 3 (Armed Guard Bunker & Tripwire Alarms): Costs $4,500. Increases income/capacity by 80% and reduces police raid arrest probabilities by half.

5. Tactical Masterclasses & Odds & Ends Strategies

Strategy A: The North Shore Bootleg Blitz

In the opening 4 weeks, refrain from initiating gang wars. Secure industrial slots in the Docks to build two Moonshine Stills, then establish two Speakeasies in Little Italy. This creates a sustainable $5,000+ weekly dirty revenue stream that funds arms purchases without raising police heat.

Strategy B: The Judicial Umbrella Protocol

Prioritize acquiring your first Dry Cleaner in Week 3. Accumulate $5,000 in clean bank balance and immediately place Judge Callahan on retainer. Once secured, any soldiers arrested during surprise police sweeps will have their indictments dismissed without costing bail money.

Strategy C: Decapitation Strike Over Turf Wars

Do not waste time fighting rival outfits street-by-street. Equip two made men with Tommy Guns and Armored Sedans. Dispatch them on an assassination contract against the rival Boss. Eliminating the Boss dissolves their entire faction instantly, leaving all their unfortified rackets ripe for annexation.
